PXD-8

8-output ESP32-S3 pixel decoder

Takes an Art-Net, sACN or DDP stream on Wi-Fi and drives up to 4,096 WS281x / SK6812 / APA102 pixels at 40 fps across eight fused, level-shifted outputs. No PC in the loop. Open firmware, flashable from the browser.
